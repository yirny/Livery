skyui-heading-rule.png is SkyUI's MCM header rule (interface/skyui/configpanel.swf,
symbol OptionsListEntry, frame "header", child headerDecor), by the SkyUI Team
(https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/12604), rendered from its vector
shape as a white alpha mask; the plugin tints it at draw time. NORDIC UI ships the
same art.
