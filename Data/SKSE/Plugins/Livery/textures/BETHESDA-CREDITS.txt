vanilla-equipped.png is the equipped-item mark of Skyrim Special Edition's own
inventory (Skyrim - Interface.bsa, interface/inventory components/inventorylists.swf,
symbol EquipIcon, frame "Equipped"), rendered from its vector shape as a white
alpha mask; the plugin tints it at draw time.

It is Bethesda Softworks' game material, redistributed here only as part of a
free mod for use with The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, as the game's EULA allows.

vanilla-checkbox.png and vanilla-checkmark.png are the box and tick mark of the
game's settings checkbox (Skyrim - Interface.bsa, interface/quest_journal.swf,
symbol SettingsOptionItem, child "checkBox"), rendered from their vector shapes
in their own colours; the plugin draws them untinted. NORDIC UI uses the same
art, so the Nordic skin shares them. Same terms as above.

vanilla-dropdown.png is the arrow of the game's settings option stepper
(interface/quest_journal.swf, symbol OptionStepperArrowBtn), rendered from its
vector shape in its own colours and turned to point down; the plugin draws it
untinted on the Vanilla and Nordic skins' dropdowns. Same terms as above.
