The piece icons in this folder are SkyUI icon art, rendered from the swf frames as
128x128 white alpha masks; the plugin tints them at draw time. They are used with their
authors' permission and under the permissions on the mod pages named below.

- skyui/icons_item_psychosteve/: every icon of SkyUI's
  interface/skyui/icons_item_psychosteve.swf
  (SkyUI, https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/12604), by PsychoSteve,
  except SkyUI 6's additions listed below, which are Jelidity's.
- I4IconAddon/icons/: every icon of interface/I4IconAddon/icons.swf of Inventory
  Interface Information Injector
  (https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/85702), by Jelidity.

Jelidity's icons, in both folders:
  clothing_backpack, clothing_cloak, scroll_spider, misc_housepart, misc_camping,
  weapon_fishingrod, misc_chitin, misc_horsetack, soulgem_tomatoempty,
  soulgem_tomatopartial, soulgem_tomatofull, soulgem_ayleidcrystalempty,
  soulgem_ayleidcrystalpartial, soulgem_ayleidcrystalfull, misc_dwarvenscrap,
  misc_elderscroll, magic_sun, magic_wind, misc_jar, magic_vampire, magic_water,
  magic_earth, misc_tool, misc_toy, misc_instrument, misc_beartrap.

Credit Jelidity when reusing Jelidity's icons; AI-made variants of them are not permitted.
