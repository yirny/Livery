nordic-corner.png and nordic-heading.png are NORDIC UI's corner knot and
heading rope by Dopesan, rendered from NORDIC UI - Interface Overhaul
(https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/49881) as white
alpha masks; the plugin tints them at draw time.

Used under the author's permissions on that page:
