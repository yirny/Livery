vanilla-equipped.png is the equipped-item mark of Skyrim Special Edition's own
inventory (Skyrim - Interface.bsa, interface/inventory components/inventorylists.swf,
symbol EquipIcon, frame "Equipped"), rendered from its vector shape as a white
alpha mask; the plugin tints it at draw time.

It is Bethesda Softworks' game material, redistributed here only as part of a
free mod for use with The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, as the game's EULA allows.
